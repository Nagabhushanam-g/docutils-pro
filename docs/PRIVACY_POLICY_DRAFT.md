DocUtils Pro is an Android document utility for scanning, cropping, converting images to PDF, basic Word/Excel-to-PDF conversion, viewing documents, and saving files on your device.

## Document and image data
Documents selected or created with the app are processed locally for the core editing/conversion features and saved on the device, including user-visible storage such as Documents/DocUtils Pro and Pictures/DocUtils Pro. We do not operate a document-upload account service or receive copies of your selected document content on our own servers as part of the described document conversion flow. Files you share through Android sharing can be sent to the third-party service you choose. Some scanner functionality is provided by Google Play services/ML Kit; Google's own practices may apply to that service.

## Ads, consent, billing and service providers
The free version uses Google AdMob, which can process advertising identifiers, device information, interactions and other data according to Google's policies. A Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow requests and stores applicable privacy choices before ad requests, and users may be offered Privacy choices in the app when required. Ad delivery and consent can require internet access.

If you buy the ad-free option, Google Play Billing processes the purchase. The app checks Play purchase status and acknowledgement to determine ad-free access. Payment information is handled by Google Play; we do not collect card details in the app.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
Google Play services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions
Camera permission may be requested to photograph or scan documents. The Android system's photo/file picker lets you choose files to convert. Internet access is used by Google advertising, consent and billing services. The app does not require a DocUtils Pro account.

## Retention and deletion
Files you save remain on your device until you delete them using DocUtils Pro or the system file manager. Uninstalling the app may not delete files saved to public Documents/Pictures folders; app-private cached data may be removed. AdMob and Google Play may retain service data under their respective privacy policies.
