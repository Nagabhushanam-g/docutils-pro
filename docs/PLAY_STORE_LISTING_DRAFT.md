# DocUtils Pro — Google Play Store listing draft

**Status:** Draft for publisher review. Do not submit until the store screenshots match the final Play release and purchases, consent, and release signing are tested.

## Main store listing

**App name:** DocUtils Pro

**Short description (max 80 characters):**
Scan, crop and convert images to PDF. Organize documents on your phone.

**Full description:**

DocUtils Pro helps you scan, prepare, convert, and organize everyday documents on your Android phone.

Turn photos into PDFs:
- Pick several images from your gallery and combine them into a single PDF.
- Detect and crop document edges automatically when possible.
- Adjust the crop manually if the document needs a closer fit.
- Reorder pages, review the images, and choose image appearance and compression settings.
- Use the camera or Smart Scan to capture documents.

Keep documents organized:
- Save PDFs and scanned images in the Documents/DocUtils Pro and Pictures/DocUtils Pro folders.
- Find recent files, search your saved documents, and organize them with local folder labels.
- View, share, rename, or delete saved documents.

Work with common Office files:
- Convert basic Word and Excel documents to PDFs on-device.
- For complex layouts, charts, or printing formats, export a PDF from your Office app first, then import it into DocUtils Pro for saving and viewing.

Core document editing and conversion works locally on your device. Some optional features, such as advertisements, Google Play purchases, and Google-provided scanning services, may use network services.

The free version includes advertisements. An ad-free one-time purchase may be available through Google Play.

## Review notes
- The Word/Excel feature is intentionally described as **basic**, not pixel-perfect conversion.
- Do not describe the app as 100% offline: AdMob, Play Billing and Google's scanner SDK can use network requests.
- Claims about automatic cropping should note that uncertain documents may remain uncropped for safety.
- Complete and verify Google Play declarations before publishing:
  https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152
  https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- Accounts subject to new-personal-account rules may need at least 12 closed-test participants opted in for 14 continuous days before applying for production:
  https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465
