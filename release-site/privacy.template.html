Local files

DocUtils Pro processes selected images and Office files on the device for its core conversion features. Converted documents are saved locally; sharing files from the Android share menu sends them to destinations you choose.

The free app integrates Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and relevant privacy choices. Google's advertising technologies may process device, advertising and usage data. Some advertising and consent features use network services. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional ad-free purchase is managed by Google Play Billing. Payment-card information is handled by Google Play, not our app.

Google scanning services

Some optional scanning features use Google's ML Kit/Play services and may require network access or software components from Google. Consult Google's privacy documentation.

Retention and choices

Files remain in your device's Documents/DocUtils Pro or Pictures/DocUtils Pro folders until you delete them. Publicly saved files may remain after uninstalling. Where applicable, you can review advertising privacy choices in the app.
